Recommended camera position

For swing mechanics, start with a face-on view: camera around hand height, level, far enough back to show your full body and club. Then record a separate down-the-line view. Keep the iPad and tripod well outside the ball's flight and rebound zone.

The camera records video only. It does not yet calculate ball speed, spin, launch angle or carry.
