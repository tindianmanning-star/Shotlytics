Mirror iPad to a monitor
  1. Connect the iPad and TV/monitor to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Control Centre: swipe down from the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring.
  4. Select your AirPlay-compatible TV or Apple TV.

This button shows the steps; Safari websites cannot start AirPlay mirroring directly. For a wired display, use a compatible Lightning-to-HDMI adapter.
