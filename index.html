Basic review checklist

Use these prompts while comparing the address, top, impact and finish frames. They are observation prompts, not automated diagnoses.

  • Setup: balanced stance, athletic knee flex, hip hinge, arms hanging naturally.
  • Top: maintain balance and posture; avoid excessive sway or loss of control.
  • Impact: observe whether your head and torso remain controlled and whether your hands are ahead of the ball where appropriate.
  • Finish: balanced finish, chest turned towards the target, weight supported on the lead side.

For a right-handed golfer, use DTL to inspect posture and swing direction, and Face-On to inspect sway, balance and weight shift. Keep the full club and body in frame.

18Birdies AI swing analysis

ShotLytics records and keeps your swing. Submit the video to 18Birdies AI Coach, then bring the complete feedback back here. This is a guided transfer workflow; direct automatic account syncing is not currently supported.

  1. Record or select your swing in ShotLytics.
  2. Use the video share/open action to send it to 18Birdies, or save it to Photos and upload it in 18Birdies AI Coach → Analyse My Swing.
  3. When 18Birdies returns its analysis, enter or paste the complete feedback below and save it against this swing.
